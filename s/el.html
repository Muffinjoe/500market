About Estée Lauder Companies (The)

Estee Lauder Companies Inc is a multinational cosmetics and skincare company that develops, manufactures, and distributes a wide range of beauty and personal care products under various brand names, including Estee Lauder, MAC, Clinique, and La Mer. The company's portfolio includes skincare, makeup, fragrance, and hair care products, catering to diverse consumer needs and preferences. As a leading player in the global beauty industry, Estee Lauder Companies Inc drives innovation and growth through its commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction.
