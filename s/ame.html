About Ametek

Ametek is a leading global manufacturer of electronic instruments and electromechanical devices with a diverse range of products serving industrial, aerospace, defense, and other markets. The company's key offerings include process analytical instruments, process control systems, and electromechanical devices, among others. Ametek's products play a critical role in various industries, enabling customers to improve efficiency, productivity, and safety.
