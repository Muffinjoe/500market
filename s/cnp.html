About CenterPoint Energy

CenterPoint Energy is a utility company that provides electric transmission and distribution services to residential, commercial, and industrial customers in the United States. The company operates in eight states, delivering electricity and natural gas to over 2.5 million metered customers. As a key player in the energy infrastructure sector, CenterPoint Energy plays a vital role in meeting the energy needs of its customers and supporting the nation's economic growth.
