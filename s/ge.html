About GE Aerospace

General Electric is a multinational conglomerate that operates in the fields of aviation, healthcare, energy, and industry, providing a range of products and services including jet engines, medical imaging equipment, and power generation equipment. The company's diverse portfolio enables it to serve a broad customer base across various industries, from aerospace and healthcare to energy and transportation.
