About Agilent Technologies

Agilent Technologies is a leading provider of life sciences, diagnostics, and applied chemical markets, offering a range of products and services including analytical instruments, software, consumables, and application expertise. The company's solutions are used in various fields such as pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, food safety, and clinical diagnostics, enabling customers to advance their research, development, and healthcare applications. By providing innovative technologies and solutions, Agilent Technologies helps drive scientific discovery, improve healthcare outcomes, and enhance quality of life.
